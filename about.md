I’m Isaac Sudweeks, a researcher and engineer interested in intelligent systems that have to interact with the real world.

My work sits at the intersection of reinforcement learning, robotics, control, and simulation. I’m especially drawn to questions where the software and the mechanics cannot be separated: how a robot’s structure shapes what it can learn, how simulation changes the experiments we can run, and how learning-based methods can become more useful on physical systems.

## This site

This is both my personal homepage and the public layer of my research notebook. The writing here comes from active work—paper breakdowns, experiments, technical explanations, limitations, and ideas worth returning to.

Most notes begin in a rough, private form and are refined just enough to be useful to someone else. I keep Markdown as the source of truth so the process stays lightweight, versioned, and close to the research itself.

## Areas I keep returning to

- reinforcement learning and model-based control
- unusual robot morphologies and embodied intelligence
- differentiable and accelerated simulation
- scientific machine learning
- tools that make research more reproducible

You can follow the work and see current projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/isaa-sudweeks).
