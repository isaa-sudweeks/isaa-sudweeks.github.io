Developed and validated the proof of concept for a portable structure that forms a surfable standing wave in a river. The design translates open-channel flow principles into a deployable geometry and establishes the foundation for full-scale testing.

03 / DATA & ML

Atmospheric Response Modeling

Created a statistical and machine-learning workflow that transforms large experimental chemistry datasets into interpretable pollutant-response surfaces, helping reveal how precursor concentrations affect ozone formation.

  • Machine learning
  • Statistics
  • Visualization
Instrumentation

04 / MECHATRONICS

Automated Atmospheric Testbed

Engineered and validated an automated instrument for continuously measuring atmospheric compounds. The system integrates sensors, fluid routing, solenoid control, data acquisition, power electronics, and a live operator interface.

  • Sensors
  • DAQ
  • GUI
Industrial automation

05 / AUTOMATION

Automated Webbing Dispenser

Built a production machine that measures and dispenses precise lengths of webbing automatically, replacing a manual workflow with encoder feedback, embedded control, serial communication, and AC motor actuation.

  • Raspberry Pi
  • Encoders
  • Motor control
Manufacturing

Designed and manufactured a working compressed-air engine across five production methods, adapting individual components for milling, turning, casting, powder metallurgy, and additive manufacturing.
